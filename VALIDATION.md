## Executed full local result

`results/benchmark_results.csv` was generated by `scripts/run_benchmark.py` from the official small ISIC Task 1 archive. The completed all-data run verifies 900 image/mask pairs, trains on 540 pairs, validates on 180 pairs, and evaluates all methods on the same 180-image held-out test split.

Measured held-out metrics from the run:

| Method | Mean Dice | Median Dice | Std Dice | Mean IoU | Samples |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Otsu | 0.611694 | 0.755241 | 0.334606 | 0.516329 | 180 |
| K-means + Morphology | 0.586146 | 0.729107 | 0.345442 | 0.492297 | 180 |
| U-Net | 0.000979 | 0.000000 | 0.003818 | 0.000493 | 180 |

The measured best method for this run is Otsu. The short CPU U-Net run successfully saved and reloaded a checkpoint, but its held-out score is poor; this is reported as measured and is not replaced with a fabricated neural-model result. A longer GPU training run and hyperparameter study would be needed before drawing conclusions about U-Net quality.

Synthetic fixtures remain available only through `scripts/run_smoke_benchmark.py` for regression testing.
